Delete Your SchoolLife Account

SchoolLife · Last updated: October 31, 2026

You can delete your SchoolLife account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open SchoolLife and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete when asked to confirm. Your account is closed straight away.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email gradexapp1@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject line "SchoolLife account deletion request". Include your name and the email address on the account. We may ask you to verify your identity, and we will confirm once your account has been deleted.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile and sign-in details (name, email, photo, password)Deleted
Courses, grades, CGPA records, and timetable entriesDeleted
Push notification tokens and registered devicesDeleted
Photos, files, and voice notes you uploaded to class chatsDeleted from our storage
Classes you createdOwnership is transferred to another admin where one exists; otherwise the class is archived or deleted
Messages you sent in classesRemoved or anonymized, so they no longer show your name or link to you
Logs and backupsRemain for a short period and are then overwritten (see below)

What may be kept

We may keep limited information where required for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons, such as records needed to resolve a dispute or investigate serious misuse. Backups may hold copies for a short period before being overwritten.

How long it takes

Your account is deactivated immediately when you confirm deletion. Your personal data is deleted or anonymized within 30 days.

This cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, we cannot recover your courses, grades, or timetable. If you want to keep a copy, export or screenshot your results before deleting.

Want to delete only some data?

You can remove individual courses, semesters, timetable entries, or chat messages inside the app without deleting your account. For other partial requests, contact gradexapp1@gmail.com.
