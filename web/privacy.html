Privacy Policy

SchoolLife · Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026

SchoolLife ("SchoolLife", "we", "us", "our") is a CGPA calculator and academic management app for students, available on Android and the web. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using SchoolLife, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

SchoolLife is operated by TRIMAX, based in Nigeria. For any privacy question or request, contact us at gradexapp1@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, profile photo (if you sign in with Google), and a password if you register with email.
  • Student profile: your matric number, school, faculty, department, and level.
  • Academic data: courses, credit units, grades, semesters, CGPA and GPA records, and any result sheets you choose to scan.
  • Timetable data: lectures, exams, and personal schedule entries you create.
  • Class and chat content: classes you create or join, your username, messages, reactions, replies, edits, photos, files, and voice notes you send in class chats, and messages you send in anonymous sessions.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support or submit feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device type, operating system version, app version, and crash or error information.
  • Push notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can deliver class alerts and announcements to your device.
  • Usage and connection data: basic server logs such as IP address, timestamps, and request information, used for security and reliability.
  • Chat status data: delivery and read status of messages, so the app can show sent, delivered, and read ticks.

Information that stays on your device

  • Biometric login: if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, the check is done by your device's operating system. We never receive, see, or store your fingerprint or face data.
  • Result sheet scanning: text recognition from camera scans runs on your device using Google ML Kit. The scanned image is not uploaded to our servers for recognition.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To calculate, store, and sync your CGPA, courses, and timetable across your devices.
  • To run classes, chat, anonymous sessions, and notifications.
  • To send service messages such as password reset emails, security alerts, and important updates.
  • To keep SchoolLife safe: preventing abuse, spam, and fraud, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To fix bugs, monitor performance, and improve the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to show you third-party advertising.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your academic data and timetable are private to your account. Other users cannot see them.
  • Class content: messages, photos, files, and voice notes you post in a class are visible to the members of that class, along with your username or name as shown in the class. Class admins can manage members and class settings.
  • Anonymous sessions: when a class admin starts an anonymous session, other members, including the admin, will not see your name next to your message. To keep the platform safe and handle abuse reports, we may keep internal records that link a message to the account that sent it, and we may use them if needed to investigate serious misuse or to comply with the law.
Please be careful about what you share in a class. Anything you post can be seen, saved, or screenshotted by other members, and we cannot control what they do with it.

6. How long we keep your information

  • We keep your account data for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited information to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.
  • Backups may keep copies for a short additional period before they are overwritten.
  • Messages you clear or that disappear under your own settings are removed from your view. Messages other class members have received may remain in their chat history.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit directly in the app).
  • Delete your data and account.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account: you can delete your account inside the app at Settings > Delete Account, or by following the steps on our Delete Your Account page. For any other request, email gradexapp1@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can also turn off push notifications, camera, microphone, and biometric access at any time in your device settings.

8. Security

We protect your data with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls, and secure hosting. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your device locked.

9. Children and students under 18

SchoolLife is built for students, and some users may be under 18. If you are under 18, you should use SchoolLife only with the permission of a parent or guardian. SchoolLife is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

SchoolLife may contain links to other websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.
