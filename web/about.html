About SchoolLife

Your student life, in one app.

SchoolLife is a CGPA calculator and academic management app built for students. It brings the things you juggle every semester into one place: your grades, your timetable, and your classmates.

What you can do with SchoolLife

Track your CGPAAdd your courses, credit units, and grades, and see your GPA for every semester and your overall CGPA.
Scan your result sheetUse your camera to read results from a result sheet instead of typing them in.
Plan your timetableKeep your lectures, exams, and personal schedule together, with reminders so you never miss a class.
Join a classJoin your class with a link, chat with classmates, share photos, files, and voice notes, and get announcements from your class admin.
Anonymous sessionsClass admins can open an anonymous session so students can speak up without their name showing.
Your data, your choiceHide your CGPA, sign in with your fingerprint, and delete your account whenever you like.

Why we built it

Students shouldn't need a calculator, a notebook, a group chat, and a pile of reminders to stay on top of school. SchoolLife is our attempt to make academic life simpler, calmer, and easier to manage, for you and your classmates.

Who is behind it

SchoolLife is designed, built, and maintained by TRIMAX. We read every message we get, and your feedback shapes what we build next.

Please note: SchoolLife is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any school or institution. CGPA and GPA results are estimates based on what you enter, so always confirm your official results with your institution.
