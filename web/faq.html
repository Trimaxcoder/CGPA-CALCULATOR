Account and sign-in

How do I create an account?

Tap Create one on the sign-in screen. Enter your name, email, and matric number, choose a password, then add your school, faculty, department, and level. You can also continue with Google.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email. We'll send you a reset link. If you're already signed in, go to Settings > Privacy & security > Change password to get a link by email. Check your spam folder if it doesn't show up.

How do I sign in with my fingerprint?

After you sign in with your email and password, SchoolLife offers to turn on fingerprint login. Accept, and next time you can use the Sign in with Fingerprint button. Your fingerprint is checked by your phone and never reaches us.

Can I change my name, school, or level?

Yes. Go to Settings and open your Profile to edit your details.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and data and can't be undone. See Delete Your Account for exactly what is removed.

CGPA and grades

How is my CGPA calculated?

Each course's grade point is multiplied by its credit units. SchoolLife adds those up across your courses and divides by your total credit units. Your semester GPA uses only that semester's courses, while your CGPA uses all of them.

Is the CGPA in the app official?

No. It's an estimate based on what you enter. Always confirm your official results and grading rules with your school.

My CGPA doesn't match my school's result. Why?

The most common causes are a wrong credit unit, a missing or repeated course, or a grading scale that differs from your school's. Double-check your courses and units. If it still looks off, contact us with the details.

How does result sheet scanning work?

Use the scanner to take a photo of your result sheet. The text is read on your phone and turned into course entries. It can make mistakes, so always check every entry before saving.

Can I hide my CGPA?

Yes. Go to Settings > Privacy & security and turn on Hide CGPA to keep it hidden on the Grades screen.

Will my data be saved if I switch phones?

Your courses and timetable are saved to your account and sync when you're online. Sign in on your new phone with the same email or Google account to get them back.

Classes and chat

How do I join a class?

Open Join a class from the More menu and use the link your class admin shared, or the join button. Some classes need the admin to approve you first.

Who can see my messages in a class?

Everyone in that class can see what you post, along with your username or name. Your CGPA, grades, and timetable stay private to you. Be careful what you share, because other members can save or screenshot messages.

What is an anonymous session?

A class admin can open an anonymous session where students send messages without their name showing. It's meant for honest questions and feedback, not for abuse. Serious misuse can still be investigated.

Can I delete or clear messages?

You can edit your messages, and clear a chat from your own view. You can also turn on disappearing messages for yourself. Messages other members have already received may stay in their chat.

How do I leave a class or report someone?

Open the class and use the members and settings screen to leave. To report abuse, email us at gradexapp1@gmail.com with the class name, the username, and what happened.

Notifications and reminders

I'm not getting notifications. What should I check?
  • In Settings > Notifications, make sure the reminders you want are switched on.
  • In your phone's settings, allow notifications for SchoolLife.
  • Turn off battery optimization or "sleeping apps" for SchoolLife, since some phones stop background alerts.
  • Sign out and back in, especially after reinstalling the app.
  • Check that Do Not Disturb isn't on.

Still stuck? Contact us with your phone model.

My timetable reminders come late. Why?

Some phones delay alerts to save battery. Allow exact alarms and turn off battery optimization for SchoolLife in your phone settings.

Privacy and data

What data does SchoolLife collect?

Your account and student profile details, your courses and grades, your timetable, and the content you post in classes. The full list is in our Privacy Policy. We don't sell your data.

Is my data safe?

We use encrypted connections and secure hosting, and we only share data with the services that run the app. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password.

How do I get a copy of my data or fix it?

You can edit most of your data in the app. For anything else, email gradexapp1@gmail.com.
